I am interested in how large language models can become a useful modality for transportation systems rather than only a text interface.

This includes geospatial representations from LLMs, LLM-empowered demand prediction, macro-aligned micro-record synthesis, and agentic systems for interpretable mobility generation.

Representative work: [LLM4TR](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ait.2025.100003), [LLM2GeoVec](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2408.12116), [LLMSynthor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14752), and [MobEvolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01640).

About

I am a PhD student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University and Tongji University, supervised by Prof. Jian Sun and Prof. Wei Ma. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, transportation systems, and autonomous driving safety.

From a micro perspective, I study how to generate, test, and train with realistic but safety-critical driving scenarios. From a macro perspective, I develop spatiotemporal models for sparse sensing, traffic forecasting, mobility generation, and urban AI.

I am currently working with the PolyU Mobility AI Lab and TOPS at Tongji University. I am especially interested in adversarial learning, LLM agents, preference alignment, and generalizable spatiotemporal representations.
